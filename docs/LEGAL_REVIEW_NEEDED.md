# Items that need legal / owner approval before publication

* **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service** (`src/content/pages.ts`) are *drafts*. Each public page shows a "needs approved legal review" banner. They contain bracketed placeholders: operating entity's legal name and address, data retention period, governing law and jurisdiction, limitation of liability. A qualified lawyer must complete them (consider Kuwait data-protection rules and, for EU/UK visitors, GDPR) and then the banner text can be removed in Admin → Texts (`draft.note`).
* **Cookie consent**: analytics load only after acceptance. Confirm the wording with the lawyer.
* **Customer stories and testimonials**: none are published. Add them only with written customer approval.
* **Claims**: every product claim is traced in `ERP_FEATURE_DISCOVERY.md`. The "300+ reports", backups and "assistant" wording should be re-checked by the product owner before launch.
* **Prices**: none are shown. Enter plans only after management approval.
* **Social accounts**: none are shown until real `https://` profile links are entered in Settings.
* **Company details** (registered name, commercial licence number, address) are not on the site because none were provided.
